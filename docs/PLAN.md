# One-page plan

**Purpose, in one sentence (the rules ask for exactly this):** Mayday talks a panicking bystander through a medical emergency, out loud and step by step, while help is on the way, even with no signal.

**Audience:** anyone standing next to someone who collapsed, is choking, or is bleeding, especially people with no CPR training. 71.3% of cardiac arrests happen at home (CARES 2024), so the person there is usually family.

**What we are betting on**, based on `research/findings.md`:

1. **One button for every emergency, chosen by voice.** Ten protocols behind one hold-to-start control. The closest past winner (HeartAngel, CA-49 2025) coaches CPR only and doesn't listen.
2. **Medicine you can check.** Every step cites its guideline; tests enforce the 2025 changes; the AI can't write instructions.
3. **Engineering you can measure.** Zero metronome drift over 10 minutes; held-out router accuracy reported, misses listed; offline by default.
4. **Restraint as design.** Liquid Glass in calm mode; black and 128 pt numbers in an emergency.

**Scope:** MVP is the 10 protocols, voice, 911, map, photo check, offline. Stretch (cut first if late): Spanish, accelerometer rate feedback, anaphylaxis/seizure/heat/hypoglycemia, lock-screen widget. **Never cut:** CPR quality, offline mode, the code explanation in the video.

**Video (3:00 max), mapped to the judging guide:**
