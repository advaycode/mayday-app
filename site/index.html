The model

    The person is simulated: chest and belly motion, a heartbeat's two vibrations, footsteps, a hand, and a phone-class sensor's noise (mobile/src/sensors/sim.ts). The model is the real one, the same TypeScript the app runs, checked against the trained PyTorch model. Experimental and lab only: Mayday never uses a heartbeat to decide anything in an emergency, the same way lay rescuers are taught not to check for a pulse.
