Mayday: a voice that talks you through a medical emergency | Congressional App Challenge 2026

**Description:**

Mayday talks a panicking bystander through a medical emergency, out loud and step by step, while help is on the way, even with no signal.

Tap one button and say what's happening, or tap the emergency. Mayday picks the right protocol and coaches you: CPR for adults, children and babies, choking, severe bleeding, burns, stroke, opioid overdose and the recovery position. During CPR it counts every compression at 110 a minute on the phone's audio clock, cues breaths and rescuer swaps, and switches to the AED the moment you say it's here. It never dials 911: you call, and it shows your address and a summary to read out. It shows the nearest emergency room and trauma center, and keeps a log for the paramedics.

Every instruction cites the 2025 American Heart Association guidelines, the American Red Cross, or another named source. The AI never writes medical advice: it only helps pick a protocol, and its answers are checked before the app can use them.
